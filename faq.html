Stays & Local Exploring

For guests who wish to stay close to the venue, there are excellent 5-star hotels located just 10 to 15 minutes north of Ramada by Wyndham along the main bypass highway. We highly recommend checking out:

Le Méridien Kochi: A beautiful waterside resort property located very close to Kumbalam.

Crowne Plaza Kochi: A highly rated luxury hotel located right on the main bypass junction.

If you would like to check out the local area, here are a few nearby highlights:

Fort Kochi View Point & Vasco da Gama Square: A scenic coastal promenade about 35 minutes from the resort. It is the perfect spot for an evening stroll to watch the sunset and see the iconic, 500-year-old Chinese fishing nets in action.

Mattancherry Palace: Located right near Fort Kochi, this historic 1555 palace (also known as the Dutch Palace) features a grand central courtyard and incredibly well-preserved traditional Kerala murals.

St. Francis CSI Church: Positioned in the heart of Fort Kochi, this quiet landmark was built in 1503 and is recognized as the oldest European church in India. It is also famous as the original burial site of the explorer Vasco da Gama.

Kerala Folklore Museum: Located just 15 minutes north of our venue, this spectacular three-story architectural masterpiece houses over 4,000 artifacts showcasing South India's traditional arts, dance masks, and history.
