Itinerary

WHERE TO BE AND WHAT TO WEAR

All events held at Ramada by Wyndham Kochi

Haldi

Saturday · December 5, 2026

Dress Code: Kerala-Inspired Haldi

Join us for an evening of turmeric blessings and heartfelt rituals! To match our traditional theme, we'd love for guests to wear classic white and gold (feel free to add a vibrant pop of color!).

  • ✧ Attire Options: Traditional Kerala-style outfits are highly encouraged, but any festive white and gold Indian wear is perfect. We love seeing fun, colorful blouses, dupattas, or accessories paired with your whites! Click here to see visual examples.
  • ✧ A Gentle Note: Please wear something comfortable that you don't mind getting a little yellow and wet!

6:00 PM

Evening Haldi

An intimate evening of tradition to start our wedding weekend. Join us for the vibrant turmeric rituals as we share music, laughter, and celebrations.

8:30 PM

Sadhya

Traditional Kerala dinner to follow the Haldi. Relax, watch the water, and enjoy the authentic flavors of the region served on banana leaves.

The Wedding

Sunday · December 6, 2026

Dress Code: South Indian Seaside

Join us in traditional Indian festive wear! To match our beautiful ocean backdrop, we'd love for guests to wear airy pastels and bright, happy shades.

  • ✧ Fabrics: Because our venue is right by the coast, light and breathable fabrics are highly recommended to stay comfortable in the seaside breeze.
  • ✧ A Gentle Request: We welcome all bright colors and soft pastels, but kindly ask that guests avoid wearing red or any vibrant shades of pink, as these colors are reserved for the bride.

8:30 AM

The Muhurtham

The sacred wedding ceremony begins bright and early. We kindly ask guests to be seated by 8:00 AM to witness the traditional rituals, share in the blessings, and celebrate our union.

11:00 AM

Lunch & Photos

Enjoy a relaxed lunch and mingle with the newlyweds. We invite all our guests to stay for group pictures to commemorate the day!

The Reception

Sunday · December 6, 2026

Dress Code: Starry Night

Get ready to dance under the stars in Indian Festive Attire! To match the evening's elegant vibe, we'd love for guests to wear deep jewel tones and colors of the galaxy.

  • ✧ Attire Options: Traditional Indian festive wear is requested, but elegant long dresses and semi-formal suits are also wonderfully suited for the evening.
  • ✧ Color Inspiration: Think of the rich, moody colors of a night sky—midnight navy, deep emerald green, rich burgundy, deep plum, and dark charcoal, accented with gold or silver.

7:00 PM

Cocktail Hour

A pre-dinner social hour with cocktails and ambient music while guests arrive and settle.

8:00 PM

Dinner & Dancing

Formal dinner, special performances, and an open dance floor. Get ready for an unforgettable night!
